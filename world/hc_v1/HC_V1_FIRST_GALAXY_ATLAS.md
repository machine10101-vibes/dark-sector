# HC-V1 ATLAS — The Dark Sector
First MMO galaxy for DARK SECTOR ONLINE. Names and jobs are locked. Scan layers and loot get filled by the galaxy loop.

Catalog: **Helion Catalog Veil 1**  
Player name: **The Dark Sector**  
Systems: 48 (8 regions × 6)  
Rule: unique flagship per region. Belts/trash/streams in every system.

IDs are stable. Do not renumber.

## Cross-region spine (build in slice G11)

Helion Dock → Brass Lantern → Lease → Towline → Haven Wheel  
Haven Wheel → Choir Gate (checkpoint)  
Lease → First Soil (homestead road)  
First Soil → Perimeter → Marchport → Black Quay  
Writ → Gyre (confiscated hulls become the Swallow)  
Spindle → Not Ours → Empty Tithe (jurisdiction hole used by smugglers)

Green spine through R1–R3. Amber work belt R2/R5. Red road R7–R8. Drift is a side hatch from everywhere via salvage rumors.

## Density checklist (use at G14)

Every system has: star, 2+ bodies or 1 flagship field, belt or ring, junk or stream, legal color, why_visit, at least one way to lose a probe, at least one way to make a living.

Every region has: one flagship, one trap, one market or port, one reason to come back after the first visit.
