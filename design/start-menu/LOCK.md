# Start menu — locked example

`LOCKED-EXAMPLE.jpg` is the locked visual example for the Dark Sector start menu.

Do not replace, crop, redraw, or swap this file when improving the menu. Open it before changing the title screen, and check the result against it.

## What to take from the picture

- The picture is the menu. A planet limb and a large ship fill the glass. Chrome stays small.
- The title sits in the open sky, centered. A small line sits over a large name.
- The ship is close and on the right, large enough that plating and a light read.
- Actions are a short, quiet stack along the bottom center. One action leads. The rest stay smaller.
- The scene stays visible around the controls. A tall panel does not cover the ship.

## What stays Dark Sector

This picture is a composition example. The menu keeps Dark Sector's own title, Helion Dock, New ship, Host, Join, Continue, and the three ships. Do not copy the example's wordmark, expansion name, login fields, or artwork into the game.
