# Locked weapon references

These three images are the visual lock for Dark Sector weapon fittings. Do not replace them.

- `turret.jpg` — projectile turret. Twin barrels, machined housing, belt feed, casings. Cheek gun, heavy turret, and stake gun take this family.
- `laser.jpg` — beam bank. Dark housing, coolant, two glowing apertures. No ammo. The beam has to connect the muzzle to the target.
- `missiles.jpg` — missile rack. Four tubes, plumbing, open mouths. Missiles are objects in flight, not a damage timer.

The pictures are proportion and material only. Keep Dark Sector names: Cheek Gun, Heavy Turret, Stake Gun, Beam Bank, Missile Rack, Point Defense. Do not copy the renders into the game as textures or meshes. Build the hardpoints in the hull code.

Each family has to change the mesh on the keel and change a fight:

- Bullets miss a fast orbit more often than a stopped hull.
- A beam bank goes dark when the capacitor is empty.
- A missile can be flakked by point defense or left behind by a burn.
