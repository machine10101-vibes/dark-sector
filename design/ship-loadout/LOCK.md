# Ship loadout — locked example

`LOCKED-EXAMPLE.jpg` is the locked visual example for the Dark Sector ship loadout.

Do not replace, crop, redraw, or swap this file when improving the ship menu. Open it before changing that screen, and check the result against it.

## What to take from the picture

- The window is split. A hardware browser sits on the left. A large ship sits on the right, inside a round glass.
- The browser has a search line, a short row of filters, and hardware grouped under headings.
- The ship is the subject of the glass: big, readable, with slot marks on the ring around it.
- One control leads. The rest of the chrome stays quiet.

## What stays Dark Sector

This picture is a composition example. The menu keeps Dark Sector's own hulls, yard modules, families, and bolt language. Do not copy the example's ship name, category names, slot icons, or artwork into the game.
