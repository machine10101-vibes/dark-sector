# Competitive gap — Dark Sector Online

Working-captain, top-down, one keel. Closer to a listen-server EVE than to a first-person Star Citizen. Ashen Reach names stay as they are. This backlog maps onto the keel, craft, heat, claims, the HC-V1 atlas, and the listen host. It does not add a pillar.

## Where the live loop already holds

| Pressure | What they do | What we already ship |

## Gaps, in the order a beta would feel them

1. **Presence.** A system should say who is on the band before you lane in. Live-Ops 2 puts an authored keel count on the helm plus the captains actually in the sim. A later pass can replace the authored number with the listen host's real guest list per system.
2. **Loss with stakes.** A cold break at a green dock is insured: half the hold stays, half sits on a named wreck. Heat at 40, or a warrant, closes the premium and the slate holds the wreck. Corp insurance and a real market price for the premium are still open.
3. **Freelance work for a new pilot.** The shakedown log is a tutorial. The jobs board is a separate posting list (Brass courier, Quay tribute) that does not steal the contract queue. Player-posted jobs are not in yet; the same `jobs` list can take them later.
4. **Careers that are not the same button.** Probe, harvest, and salvage exist. They do not yet pay different standing, heat, or travel risk by career. Trade at a green capital is the next buoy, not this slice.
5. **Shared claims.** Agent ids and a crack timer exist. A crew tag and an ACL on the core are the stub still waiting; do not block the crack test to add them.
6. **Force projection.** Lanes already refuse to jump while craft are out, and a lost craft keeps kilometers. Jump fuel, alliance fleets, and sovereignty timers are not this game yet.

## Explicitly not cloning

No region-wide alliance map, no skill queue, no FPS multicrew, no second veil. Mutations stay standing, heat, prices, patrols, claim law, and rumors.
